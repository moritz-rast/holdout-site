How it works

  1. Step 1

    Choose

    Pick a challenge and an entry, from €25.

  2. Step 2

    Work out

    Do your daily workout; reps are counted on your phone by on-device pose tracking.

  3. Step 3

    Finish

    Finish all 21 days and get at least your entry back, plus a share of what remains from the entries of those who didn't finish.

Miss a day and you lose your entry, unless you withdraw with a doctor's note.

Fair by design

  • Whether you finish depends only on your own performance
  • Finish and you always get at least your entry back
  • Adults 18+ in Austria only, ID check before your first entry
  • Every result can be reviewed by a human
  • Ill or injured? Withdraw with a doctor's note and get your entry refunded
  • No top-ups, no deposits – money only moves for a challenge you join
  • Holdout's fee is 12% of the pool. It comes only from the entries of those who didn't finish and never exceeds them. If everyone finishes, there's no fee. Shown before you pay

FAQ

When does it launch?

We're in development and will launch in Austria soon.

Who can join?

Adults aged 18 or over who live in Austria.

Is my video uploaded?

Reps are counted on your phone, and by default no video leaves it. A short clip is uploaded only if you report a miscount or opt in to help improve counting.

What happens if I don't finish?

You lose that entry. Holdout's fee comes out of the entries of those who didn't finish, and the rest is shared among those who finished.

Can I change my mind after joining?

Until joining closes, you can leave for free and get your entry back in full. After that, you can only withdraw if you're ill or injured, with a doctor's note.

What if a rep is counted wrong?

The automatic count can be wrong. Report it in the app up to 24 hours after the challenge ends, and a human reviews it within 48 hours.

How are payouts made?

Through our payment provider to a bank account in your own name.
