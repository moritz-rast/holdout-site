How it works

  1. Step 1

    Choose

    Choose a challenge and an entry (€25–€100).

  2. Step 2

    Work out

    Do your daily workout; reps are counted on your phone by on-device pose tracking.

  3. Step 3

    Finish

    Finish all 21 days and get at least your entry back, plus a share of the entries of those who didn't finish.

The challenges

Split into as many sets as you like. Every day for 21 days.

  • Push-ups

    40 a day

  • Squats

    60 a day

  • Plank

    2:00 a day

Fair by design

  • Results depend only on your own performance
  • Finish and you always get at least your entry back
  • Holdout keeps 12% of the pool – shown before you pay
  • Adults 18+ in Austria only, ID check before your first entry
  • Every result can be reviewed by a human
  • Ill or injured? Withdraw with a doctor's note and get your entry refunded
  • No top-ups, no deposits – money only moves for a challenge you join

FAQ

When does it launch?

We're in development and will launch in Austria soon.

Who can join?

Adults aged 18 or over who live in Austria.

Is my video uploaded?

Reps are counted on your phone. We don't upload raw video.

What happens if I don't finish?

You lose that entry. It's shared among those who finished, minus Holdout's 12%.

How are payouts made?

Through our payment provider to a bank account in your own name.
